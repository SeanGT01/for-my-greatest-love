To my favorite person,

To my Baby,

I don't think you realize just how much you've come to mean to me. Somewhere along the way, you became more than just someone I love talking to—you became someone who makes my days feel lighter, my smiles a little more genuine, and my life a lot more beautiful just by being in it.

There's something about you that makes even the simplest moments feel special. Whether we're laughing about something stupid, talking about our day, or simply spending time together, I find myself wishing those moments could last a little longer. Being with you has become one of my favorite feelings.

Before I even realized it, you became someone I looked forward to talking to every day. Someone I wanted to tell about the little things that happened, someone I wanted to spend my time with, and someone who somehow made even the most ordinary days feel a little more special.

And I know things between us haven't always been perfect. We've had our rough moments, misunderstandings, and times when things weren't easy. But even through those moments, you still tried to understand me, stayed patient with me, and found it in your heart to forgive me when I made mistakes. I know that isn't always easy, and I want you to know how much I appreciate it. It means more to me than I probably say enough.

Looking back at all the memories we've made, I realized that what makes them so special isn't really where we went or what we did. It's the fact that I got to experience all of those moments with you.

Thank you for coming into my life and becoming such a special part of it. Thank you for every conversation, every laugh, every random moment, every time you understood me, and even the smallest memories that probably didn't seem important at the time but ended up meaning so much to me.

I don't know exactly what the future has waiting for us, but I do know that I'm really grateful that our paths crossed and that our story started the way it did.

I made this little website for you not because today has to be some big or special occasion, but because sometimes I feel like words alone aren't enough to show you how much you truly mean to me.

I love you so much, baby. Please always take care of yourself, especially during the times when I can't be there beside you. And whenever life feels heavy, whenever you're tired, scared, sad, or you simply feel like the world is too much, please remember that you never have to face everything alone. I'm here. I'll listen to you, comfort you, support you, and stay by your side through the good days and the difficult ones.

Thank you for loving me even when I'm not the easiest person to love. Thank you for understanding me, forgiving me, and still choosing to stay through the moments when things between us weren't perfect. I may not always have the right words, and I may make mistakes along the way, but please never doubt how much I love and care about you.

And sometimes I think about how different life could have been if even one small thing had happened differently. Maybe we would have never met, never had that first conversation, and never made all the memories we have now. That thought makes me even more grateful that somehow, in this life, I found you.

And if there really are countless universes out there—countless versions of us living completely different lives—I hope that somehow, in every single one of them, I still find my way to you. No matter where we are, who we become, or how differently our stories begin, I hope my heart still recognizes yours.

Because if I were given a thousand lives, a thousand different beginnings, and a thousand chances to love someone all over again, I would still wish for the same person.

I would still wish for you.

In this universe, in another, and in every life after this one—I hope it will always be you.

I love you, baby. More than these words could ever fully explain. Please take care of yourself, always. And whenever you need someone, remember that I'm here for you. Always. ❤️

With all my love,

Your favorite person. 🌿
